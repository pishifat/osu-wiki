osu! has introduced the breakcore genre to countless players. **dream1x** is one of them.

![](https://assets.ppy.sh/artists/576/header.jpg)

Drawing influence from renowned breakcore musicians like [*goreshit*](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/57) and [*Cynthoni*](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/169) (previously known as *Sewerslvt*), [Estonian player](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/11764140) and producer **dream1x** has developed his own take on the genre featuring a haunting atmosphere and fast-paced percussion.

The emotion behind music is experienced differently by each listener, but here's how **dream1x** describes it:

> I aim to evoke a sense of nostalgia, melancholy, and motion—tracks that make you feel like you're racing through a dream you don't want to wake up from.

Enough words. Go to [**dream1x**'s Featured Artist listing](https://osu.ppy.sh/beatmaps/artists/576) to find **6** pre-timed beatmap templates, and map your favourite.
